Request deletion of some Clenzo data

You can ask us to delete specific data without deleting your Clenzo account. Email clenzo.india@gmail.com from your Clenzo sign-in email. Tell us which vehicle, photo, apartment selection, or other information you want removed. Do not send a password.

We will verify account ownership and explain what we can delete. Some service and payment-status records may need to be retained while an obligation or dispute remains open, or for a period required by law. We will explain any retention in our response.

To delete the whole account, use the account deletion instructions.
